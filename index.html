🚇 Senior-Friendly Transit & Nav Cheat Sheet

💳 Tap & Go IC Cards

Add digital ICOCA or Suica to Apple Wallet (or buy physical ICOCA card). Tap at all ticket gates for subways, JR trains, and buses.

🛗 Look for "EV" Signs

All Osaka train stations have barrier-free elevators from platform to concourse and street. Follow the yellow "EV" icons to skip stairs completely.

🔴 Midosuji Line (M)

The red spine of Osaka. Connects Dobutsuen-mae, Daikokucho, Namba, Shinsaibashi, Umeda, and Tennoji (Harukas 300).

🟤 Sakaisuji Line (K)

Brown line. Connects Dobutsuen-mae, Ebisucho (Den Den Town South), and Nipponbashi (Kuromon Market).

🟢 Chuo Line (C)

Green line. Connects Sakaisuji-Hommachi directly west to Osakako (Kaiyukan Aquarium & Tempozan Harbor).

⭕ JR Osaka Loop Line (O)

Circular train line. From Shin-Imamiya connects directly to Morinomiya (Osaka Castle) and Nishikujo (transfer for USJ).
